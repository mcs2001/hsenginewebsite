Refrigeration Fault Troubleshooting Matrix

Common commercial refrigeration breakdown indicators and our engineered solutions.

Symptom Observed Potential Root Cause Impact Level HS Engineering Solution
Temperature Rising / Warm Room Refrigerant gas leak or compressor valve fatigue Critical Nitrogen pressure test, leak seal, gas recharge & valve replacement
Evaporator Coil Icing Up Defrost heater failure or blocked expansion valve Warning Defrost element replacement, expansion valve calibration & coil flush
Excessive Compressor Noise Oil breakdown or mechanical bearing wear Critical Oil analysis, crankcase heater check, or full compressor overhaul
